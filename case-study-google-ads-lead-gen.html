Colorado's Best Roofing

Google Ads Audit & Strategic Restructure

PPC Realignment, Server-Side Tracking, and Lead Qualification Architecture.

← Back to Portfolio

🔴 The Problem

  • Wasted Ad Spend: The previous campaigns were bleeding budget on high-CPC ($42+), broad-match queries like "free roof inspection," resulting in an unsustainable $285 Cost Per Lead (CPL).
  • Missing Trust Signals: Ad copy failed to leverage the company's strongest assets against "storm chaser" competitors, omitting their 40 years of local experience, GAF Master Elite status, and A+ BBB rating.
  • Broken Analytics: The tracking infrastructure lacked offline conversion tracking for inbound phone calls and suffered from severe data loss due to iOS ad blockers and unconsented traffic.

🟡 The Solution

  • Segmented Intent Campaigns: Restructured the Google Ads account into isolated, high-intent campaigns focusing on Local Services Ads (LSA) for trust, Urgent Repair/Leaks for fast conversions, and B2B Commercial queries for high-ticket ROI.
  • Ad Asset Optimization: Built out missing ad extensions, including Lead Forms with pre-qualifying questions, Price Extensions (0% Financing), and Callouts highlighting dual-manufacturer certifications (GAF & CertainTeed).
  • Advanced Tracking Infrastructure: Deployed Server-Side Google Tag Manager (GTM) and Google Consent Mode V2 to recover lost conversion data, alongside CallRail Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI) to map specific keyword data to offline phone calls.

🟢 The Expected Results

  • Reduced Cost Per Lead (CPL): Driving CPL down to the $200–$230 target range by eliminating broad-match waste and dominating cheaper, long-tail commercial queries.
  • Higher Conversion Rates: Achieving a projected 15%–18% conversion rate on urgent repair and mobile click-to-call campaigns by answering immediate, post-storm search intent.
  • 100% Attribution Accuracy: Providing clear, full-funnel visibility so the client can trace every signed roofing contract directly back to the exact search keyword that triggered the initial phone call or form submission.
