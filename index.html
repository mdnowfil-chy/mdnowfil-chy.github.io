Meta CAPI & Server-Side Tracking

Urban Jungle
▼

🔴 The Problem: Data Loss & Inflated Acquisition Costs

Following iOS 14.5+ and browser tracking preventions, the client's browser-only Meta Pixel dropped a significant portion of checkout events. The algorithm was starved of data, leading to misattributed sales and rapidly inflating CPA.

🟡 The Solution: Dual-Layer Tracking Architecture

Configured a custom web container to route interactions to a Stape.io server container. Established a direct server-to-server connection to Meta (CAPI) and hashed rich first-party data (SHA-256) to drastically improve Event Match Quality. Assigned unique Event IDs to all hits for perfect deduplication.

🟢 The Results

  • 38% Data Recovery: Captured over a third of conversion events previously blocked by privacy browsers.
  • 100% Deduplication Rate: Achieved perfect synchronization between Pixel and API events.
  • 19% CPA Reduction: Lowered acquisition costs by nearly 20% within the first 30 days.

Premium E-Commerce Meta Ads Funnel

High-End Plant Brand
▼

🔴 The Problem: High Friction & Cart Abandonment

Selling premium, live plants online introduces massive consumer anxiety regarding shipping damage. The brand suffered from high CPC and massive cart abandonment as users were hesitant to risk purchasing fragile live goods online.

🟡 The Solution: Trust-Engineered Funnel Architecture

Deployed an Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) layered with high-value Custom Lookalike Audiences. Engineered a MOF retargeting sequence utilizing an "Arrives Alive" guarantee, showing the packaging process to neutralize objections, followed by aggressive BOF dynamic product ads.

🟢 The Results

  • Overcame Buyer Hesitation: The MOF strategy successfully neutralized the primary purchasing objection.
  • Increased Conversion Rate: Significantly reduced cart abandonment from TOF traffic.
  • Profitable Scaling: Established a highly profitable ROAS, allowing for aggressive daily budget scaling.

Advanced Google Ads Conversion Tracking

Data Infrastructure
▼

🔴 The Problem: Data Discrepancy & Bidding Inefficiency

Due to strict data privacy regulations, the client faced a severe data discrepancy between backend sales and Google Ads reporting. Missing conversion data caused Google's Smart Bidding algorithms to optimize inefficiently, wasting ad spend.

🟡 The Solution: Resilient First-Party Data Integration

Built a custom GTM data layer mapped to GA4 e-commerce schemas. Implemented Google Enhanced Conversions by hashing first-party data (SHA-256) in the browser, and deployed Consent Mode v2 to dynamically adjust tag behavior based on user consent.

🟢 The Results

  • < 2% Discrepancy: Bridged the gap between backend sales and ad reporting.
  • Recovered Attribution: Successfully attributed previously "lost" conversions to specific campaigns.
  • Algorithm Empowerment: Provided Smart Bidding with high-quality signals to lower CPA.
