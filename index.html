Featured Case Studies

Meta CAPI & Server-Side Tracking

Client: Urban Jungle

🔴 The Problem

Following iOS 14.5+ and browser tracking preventions, the client's browser-only Meta Pixel dropped a significant portion of checkout events. The algorithm was starved of data, leading to misattributed sales and rapidly inflating CPA.

🟡 The Solution

Engineered a dual-layer tracking architecture. Configured a web container to route interactions to a Stape.io server container. Established a direct server-to-server connection to Meta (CAPI) and hashed rich first-party data (SHA-256) to drastically improve Event Match Quality, utilizing unique Event IDs for 100% deduplication.

🟢 The Results

38% Data Recovery
100% Deduplication
19% CPA Reduction
