GA4 E-Commerce & Full-Funnel Performance Dashboard

I built an interactive e-commerce performance dashboard in Looker Studio using Google Analytics 4 (GA4) data to analyze traffic acquisition, funnel drop-offs, and item-scoped sales.

← Back to Portfolio

🔴 The Challenge

Marketing data is often siloed, making it difficult for stakeholders to pinpoint which acquisition channels generate the highest ROI, where users abandon their shopping carts, and which specific products drive the most revenue.

🟡 The Solution & Key Implementations

I built a centralized, dynamic dashboard featuring four core analytical pillars:

  • Traffic & Revenue Attribution (Heatmap Analysis): Developed a granular matrix tracking dimensions like Session default channel group against crucial metrics including Sessions, Engagement rate, Transactions, and Purchase revenue. This instantly identifies high-converting channels (e.g., Direct and Organic Search) versus underperforming traffic.
  • Acquisition Momentum (Time-Series Tracking): Implemented trend-line visualizations to monitor daily session spikes across individual channels, allowing for the precise measurement of seasonal trends and campaign launches over 30 days.
  • Checkout Drop-Off Funnel: Engineered a custom regex-filtered visualization to map the exact user journey: view_item >> add_to_cart >> begin_checkout >> purchase. This highlights the steepest drop-off points in the funnel, providing a clear roadmap for UX and CRO improvements.
  • Item-Scoped Product Performance: Successfully navigated GA4's complex scope requirements to align item-level dimensions (Item name) with item-level metrics (Items viewed, Items added to cart, Item revenue). This section provides deep merchandising insights, revealing exactly which products capture interest versus which actually convert to sales.

🟢 The Impact

This reporting architecture eliminates data fragmentation. By visually bridging the gap between initial clicks and final revenue, it empowers data-driven decision-making, allowing marketing teams to scale profitable channels.
