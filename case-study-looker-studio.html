GA4 E-Commerce Looker Studio Dashboard

GA4, Looker Studio, Regex, and Executive Data Visualization.

← Back to Portfolio

🔴 The Problem

Stakeholders and C-suite executives were overwhelmed by the native, often complex GA4 interface. They were unable to quickly answer critical business questions—specifically identifying which traffic sources drove the highest lifetime value, and pinpointing exactly where users were abandoning the checkout flow.

🟡 The Solution

Developed a comprehensive, automated Looker Studio dashboard that democratized complex data for non-technical stakeholders.

Granular Traffic Attribution: Built custom tables comparing first-user acquisition against session-level attribution, clearly highlighting the true ROI of different marketing channels.
Item-Scoped Analytics: Visualized specific product performance, identifying top-sellers versus high-view/low-purchase items.
Regex Checkout Funnels: Utilized custom Regular Expressions (Regex) to map distinct URLs and build visual checkout drop-off funnels, isolating friction points step-by-step.

🟢 The Results

Data democratization led directly to actionable business optimizations.

  • Instant Visibility: Reduced reporting and data-gathering time by over 10 hours a month.
  • Actionable Insights: The regex funnels identified a specific friction point in the payment selection step, leading to UI changes that immediately lifted the overall conversion rate.
