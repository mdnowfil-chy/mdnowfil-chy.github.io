🔴 The Problem

🟡 The Solution

Built the Austin Digital brand from the ground up to serve as a live case study of the agency's capabilities.

Mobile-First Architecture: Developed a lightweight, lightning-fast website optimized specifically for mobile devices, ensuring perfect alignment with Google's mobile-first indexing.
Core Web Vitals Mastery: Executed aggressive technical SEO, image optimization, and code minification to achieve perfect 90+ scores across all Core Web Vitals metrics.
Instagram Organic Growth Engine: Designed a highly shareable, data-backed carousel content strategy focused on actionable marketing tactics, bypassing the need for immediate paid lead generation.
