Data Infrastructure

Advanced Google Ads Conversion Tracking

Google Tag Manager, GA4, Enhanced Conversions (SHA-256), and Consent Mode v2.

← Back to Portfolio

🔴 The Problem

Due to increasingly strict data privacy regulations and the deprecation of third-party cookies, the client faced a severe data discrepancy between their actual backend sales and Google Ads reporting. Because Google's Smart Bidding algorithms were missing critical conversion data, the campaigns were optimizing inefficiently and wasting ad spend.

🟡 The Solution

Engineered a bulletproof tracking architecture designed to respect user privacy while maximizing data fidelity.

Custom GTM Data Layer: Built a bespoke data layer mapped perfectly to GA4 e-commerce schemas to capture item-scoped purchase data.
Enhanced Conversions: Implemented Google Enhanced Conversions, hashing first-party customer data (SHA-256) in the browser before securely sending it to Google to match offline conversions to ad clicks.
Consent Mode v2 Integration: Deployed Google Consent Mode to dynamically adjust tag behavior based on user consent, allowing Google to accurately model unconsented conversions.

🟢 The Results

The new infrastructure immediately bridged the gap between raw sales and ad attribution.

  • < 2% Discrepancy: Bridged the gap between backend sales and ad reporting, reducing data discrepancy to under 2%.
  • Recovered Attribution: Successfully attributed previously 'lost' conversions back to specific Google Ads campaigns.
  • Algorithm Empowerment: Provided Google's Smart Bidding with the high-quality signals required to aggressively lower the Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).
